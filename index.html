About

I study architecture, and I have been building in Minecraft by hand for more than ten years.

I also have five years with WorldEdit and VoxelSniper, two with Axiom, and four years of media design, Blender and SketchUp. I build in 1.8.9 and in modern versions.

I ran my own Minecraft server, where I built the spawn and set up the mini games.

I have almost 300k score in Hypixel Build Battle.

I work well in a team. I have built with big groups for YouTube videos and events, on servers, and one on one with other builders. I can work 30 hours a week.
